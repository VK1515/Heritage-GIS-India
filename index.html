Map layers

Use the layer control to switch heritage monuments, earthquake epicentres, earthquake heat context, supplied fault segments and building records.

Background: OpenStreetMap tiles. Earthquake layers are derived from the uploaded earthquake catalogue; they are not an official seismic zonation map.

How to use

  1. Zoom into a region and select a monument marker for its source attributes.
  2. Toggle earthquake epicentres and the earthquake density overlay to inspect catalogue context.
  3. Switch on supplied fault segments to view the endpoint-based fault lines.
  4. Search by monument name or filter by state.

Research interpretation

This is a desktop GIS screening viewer, not a structural safety assessment. Earthquake proximity and event density do not directly represent site-specific ground motion or building vulnerability. Fault lines are approximate segments reconstructed from supplied endpoints. Verify all source records before research conclusions.
